The useful art of returning

Reading
Why a second reading is often where the interesting work begins.
Published

October 15, 2026

A passage changes when we return to it. The words are the same, but the questions we bring have become more precise. A note left in the margin is a small invitation to our future self.

Keep a question

Instead of recording everything, keep the question that made you pause. It gives the next reading a place to begin.
